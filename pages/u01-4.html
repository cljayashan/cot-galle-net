1.4Analog and digital signals

  • Analog signals vary continuously (the human voice, radio waves).
  • Digital signals use discrete levels, typically 0 and 1 (computer data).

Digital signals resist noise better because a receiver only needs to decide between two levels. Modulation converts digital data into an analog carrier for transmission over radio or telephone lines; a modem (modulator-demodulator) does this.

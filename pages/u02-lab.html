2.10Hands-on lab: Build a small star network

Do these tasks on your own computer or in the lab using free software. Write down what you see at each step: your notes are useful evidence for assessments and for revision.

  1. In Packet Tracer, place one switch and four PCs; connect them with straight-through cables.
  2. Assign IP addresses 192.168.1.1 to 192.168.1.4 with mask 255.255.255.0.
  3. Ping between all PCs.
  4. Disconnect one PC cable and observe that others still work. Explain why this shows the advantage of the star topology.
  5. Sketch the physical and logical topology of the real network at your institution.

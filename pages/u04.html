Unit 4

Addressing Network Devices

How devices are addressed: MAC addresses, binary, IPv4 classes and private ranges, subnet masks, subnetting, VLSM and IPv6.

What you will learn

After this unit you can:

  • Describe the structure and purpose of a MAC address.
  • Explain IPv4 and IPv6 addressing, classes, public and private ranges.
  • Convert between binary and decimal.
  • Design and calculate subnets using subnet masks, CIDR and VLSM.

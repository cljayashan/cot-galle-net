LayerTypical faultsChecks

Quick story

"Email fails." Ping to the mail server's IP works → not Physical/Network. nslookup mail.school.lk fails → DNS (application helper) before you rebuild SMTP. Always separate path problems from name problems from service problems.

Symptoms by layer (practical view)

LayerTypical symptomsFirst checks
PhysicalNo link light, intermittent disconnectsCable, port, power, Wi-Fi radio
Data linkLinked but no MAC learning / VLAN mismatchCorrect VLAN, switchport mode, MAC table
NetworkCannot ping gateway or remote netsIP, mask, gateway, routing
TransportPing works, app port failsFirewall ports, listening service (ss)
ApplicationPort open but app errorsURL, DNS name, app logs, certificates

A fault at a lower layer breaks everything above it. That is why "blinky lights first" saves time when the whole PC seems dead on the network.

Remember

  • Map the symptom to a layer, then test at that layer.
  • Connectivity (ping) succeeding does not prove the application layer is healthy.

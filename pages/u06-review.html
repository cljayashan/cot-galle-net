Self-check questions

Write your answers first, then open the answers to check them.

  1. Define a VPN and give its three security benefits.
  2. Differentiate remote-access and site-to-site VPNs.
  3. Why is PPTP no longer recommended?
  4. Does a VPN make a malware-infected computer safe? Explain.
Show answers

  1. A VPN is an encrypted tunnel across an untrusted network; benefits are confidentiality, integrity and authentication.

  2. Remote-access connects individual users to a network; site-to-site joins two networks through gateways.

  3. PPTP's authentication (MS-CHAPv2) and encryption (MPPE, based on RC4) have well-known weaknesses: a captured login can be cracked quickly and the traffic then decrypted. It is obsolete and should be replaced by WireGuard, OpenVPN or IPsec (IKEv2).

  4. No. A VPN protects data in transit only; the infected computer can still spread malware through the tunnel.

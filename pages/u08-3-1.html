8.3.1Symmetric encryption

The same secret key encrypts and decrypts.

  • Algorithms: AES (128/192/256-bit, the current standard), ChaCha20; older DES and 3DES are obsolete.
  • Advantages: very fast, efficient for large data (files, disks, VPN traffic).
  • Disadvantage: the key distribution problem: how do two parties share the key securely? With n users who each need private channels, n(n-1)/2 keys are required.

Self-check questions

Write your answers first, then open the answers to check them.

  1. How does a switch differ from a hub?
  2. At which OSI layer does a router operate and what address does it use?
  3. List the DORA steps of DHCP.
  4. Name the PDU at layers 4, 3 and 2.
  5. Give two applications that prefer UDP and explain why.
  6. Which TCP/IP layer corresponds to OSI layers 5 to 7?
  7. Why is Telnet considered insecure, and what replaces it?
Show answers

  1. A hub repeats frames to all ports (one collision domain, half-duplex); a switch learns MAC addresses and forwards only to the destination port (separate collision domains, full-duplex).

  2. Layer 3 (Network); IP address.

  3. Discover, Offer, Request, Acknowledge.

  4. Layer 4: segment (TCP) or datagram (UDP); layer 3: packet; layer 2: frame.

  5. VoIP and video streaming (also DNS queries, online games): they need low delay and tolerate small loss, so retransmission would hurt.

  6. The Application layer.

  7. Telnet sends everything, including passwords, in clear text; SSH replaces it with encryption.

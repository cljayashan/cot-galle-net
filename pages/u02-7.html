2.7Cabling

Twisted pair Ethernet cables are wired to either the T568A or T568B standard.

  • Straight-through cable: same standard at both ends. Connects unlike devices (PC to switch).
  • Crossover cable: T568A at one end and T568B at the other. Connects like devices (PC to PC, switch to switch), though most modern devices use Auto-MDIX and do not need it.

Maximum UTP segment length is 100 metres.

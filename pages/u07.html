Unit 7

Domain Name System (DNS)

The Domain Name System: its hierarchy, how names are resolved, record types, configuring BIND9 and Windows DNS, and registering a domain.

What you will learn

After this unit you can:

  • Explain the purpose of DNS and describe its hierarchical architecture.
  • Describe the name-resolution process and record types.
  • Install, configure and manage a DNS server.
  • Explain how to obtain and register a domain name.

1.9Multiplexing and switching

  • Multiplexing lets many signals share one link: FDM (by frequency), TDM (by time slot), WDM (by light wavelength on fibre).
  • Circuit switching: a dedicated path is set up for the whole call (traditional telephone).
  • Packet switching: data is split into packets that travel independently and are reassembled (the Internet). It uses links efficiently and tolerates failures.

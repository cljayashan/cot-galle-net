2.4Network topologies

Topology describes how devices are arranged. Physical topology is the actual cabling layout; logical topology is how data flows.

TopologyDescriptionAdvantagesDisadvantages
BusAll devices on one shared cableCheap, little cableCable fault stops everyone; collisions
RingEach device connects to two neighboursOrderly accessOne break can disrupt the ring
StarAll devices connect to a central switchEasy to manage, fault isolationCentral device is a single point of failure
Mesh (full or partial)Devices interconnect directlyVery reliableExpensive; full mesh needs n(n-1)/2 links
Tree / HybridCombination of the aboveScalableComplex

The star topology with a switch is by far the most common in modern LANs.

Worked example: how many links does a full mesh of 6 devices need? 6 x 5 / 2 = 15 links.

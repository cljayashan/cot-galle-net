Unit 3 · Network Devices and Protocols

3.4.3Encapsulation

When sending, each layer adds its own header: data becomes a segment (transport header with ports), then a packet (IP header with IP addresses), then a frame (MAC header and CRC trailer), then bits. The receiver reverses the process (de-encapsulation).

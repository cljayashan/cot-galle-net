2.1What is a computer network?

A computer network is a set of devices (hosts) connected by links so that they can share resources and exchange data. Main purposes:

  • Resource sharing: files, printers, internet connection.
  • Communication: email, messaging, voice and video.
  • Centralised management: one place to manage users, security and backups.
  • Reliability and scalability: redundancy and the ability to grow.

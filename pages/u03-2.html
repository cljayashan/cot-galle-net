3.2Install and configure network devices

This section walks through setting up the devices in a small network: planning, first-time access through the console, basic switch and router configuration, VLANs, a wireless access point, and the commands that check your work. The examples use the Cisco IOS command line, which you can practise for free in Cisco Packet Tracer.

8.5Digital certificate

How do you know a public key really belongs to the claimed owner? A digital certificate (standard X.509) binds a public key to an identity (a person, organisation or domain), and is signed by a Certificate Authority (CA).

A certificate contains: subject name (for example www.example.com), the subject's public key, issuer (the CA), validity dates, serial number, signature algorithm and the CA's digital signature.

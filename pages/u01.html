Unit 1

Data Communication Fundamentals

How data moves between devices: the parts of a communication system, data flow, signals, transmission media, performance, error detection, multiplexing and switching.

What you will learn

After this unit you can:

  • Define data communication and list its five components.
  • Distinguish transmission modes, data flow directions and signal types.
  • Compare transmission media.
  • Explain bandwidth, throughput, latency and the basic ideas of multiplexing and switching.

Unit 4 · Addressing Network Devices

4.1MAC address

A MAC (Media Access Control) address is a 48-bit hardware address burned into a network interface and used for delivery on the local network (Layer 2).

  • Written as 12 hexadecimal digits: 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E or 00-1A-2B-3C-4D-5E.
  • The first 24 bits are the OUI (Organisationally Unique Identifier) assigned to the manufacturer; the last 24 bits identify the individual card.
  • FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF is the broadcast MAC address.
  • ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) discovers the MAC address that belongs to a known IP address on the same LAN.

Find your MAC address: Windows ipconfig /all (Physical Address); Linux ip link.

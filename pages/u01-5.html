1.5Serial and parallel transmission

  • Parallel: several bits sent at once on separate wires. Fast over very short distances, but suffers from skew and cost.
  • Serial: bits sent one after another on one channel. Used for almost all long-distance and modern links (USB, SATA, Ethernet).
  • Synchronous transmission uses a shared clock and sends blocks of data; asynchronous transmission sends characters framed by start and stop bits.

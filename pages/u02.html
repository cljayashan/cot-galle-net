Unit 2

Computer Network Fundamentals

What a network is and how networks are classified by size and role, topologies, the Internet, intranets and extranets, Ethernet and Wi-Fi, cabling and network design.

What you will learn

After this unit you can:

  • Define a computer network and describe its purposes.
  • Classify networks by size (PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN) and by role (peer-to-peer, client-server).
  • Compare physical and logical topologies.
  • Explain the Internet, intranets and extranets.
  • Describe wired and wireless LAN technologies and basic network design.

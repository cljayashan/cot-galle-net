8.5.1PKI and certificate types

Public Key Infrastructure (PKI)

PKI is the system of CAs, registration authorities, certificates, revocation lists (CRL/OCSP) and policies that issues and manages certificates. Browsers and operating systems contain a list of trusted root CAs. A chain of trust runs from the server certificate to an intermediate CA to a trusted root CA.

Certificate types

  • Domain Validated (DV): proves control of a domain; quick and cheap (for example Let's Encrypt, which is free).
  • Organisation Validated (OV) / Extended Validation (EV): CA also verifies the organisation.
  • Self-signed: signed by itself; fine for labs and internal testing, but browsers show warnings.
  • Wildcard (*.example.com) and multi-domain (SAN) certificates.

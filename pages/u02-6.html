2.6Ethernet and LAN technologies

Ethernet (IEEE 802.3) is the dominant wired LAN technology. Common standards:

StandardSpeedTypical medium
10BASE-T10 MbpsCat3/Cat5 UTP
100BASE-TX (Fast Ethernet)100 MbpsCat5 UTP
1000BASE-T (Gigabit)1 GbpsCat5e/Cat6 UTP
10GBASE-T10 GbpsCat6a UTP

Ethernet originally used CSMA/CD (listen, send, detect collisions). Modern full-duplex switched Ethernet has no collisions.

Wireless LAN (IEEE 802.11, Wi-Fi): uses CSMA/CA because wireless devices cannot detect collisions while transmitting. Key concepts: SSID (network name), access point (AP), channels (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, plus 6 GHz in Wi-Fi 6E), and security modes (WPA2, WPA3; avoid the obsolete WEP).

Which Wi-Fi band? 2.4 GHz reaches further and passes through walls better, but it is slower and crowded; 5 GHz and 6 GHz are faster with more channels, but have a shorter range.

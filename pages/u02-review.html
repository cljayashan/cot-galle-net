Self-check questions

Write your answers first, then open the answers to check them.

  1. Name three purposes of a computer network.
  2. Give two advantages of client-server over peer-to-peer networks.
  3. Why is a star topology preferred in modern LANs? What is its main weakness?
  4. How many links are needed for a full mesh of 10 devices?
  5. Which cable would you use to connect a switch to another switch on older equipment?
  6. Distinguish an intranet from an extranet.
Show answers

  1. Resource sharing, communication, centralised management/security (also scalability and reliability).

  2. Central management and security; easier backup; better scalability and control of resources.

  3. Easy to manage and a fault on one cable affects only one device; weakness: the central switch is a single point of failure.

  4. 10 x 9 / 2 = 45 links.

  5. A crossover cable (modern switches with Auto-MDIX accept a straight-through cable).

  6. An intranet is private for an organisation's own users; an extranet extends controlled access to selected outside partners.

Unit 8

Network Security

Protecting servers and networks: hardening, firewalls and DMZs, symmetric and asymmetric encryption, hashing, digital signatures and certificates.

What you will learn

After this unit you can:

  • Explain the significance of security settings on servers and networks.
  • Describe the types of firewall and configure basic firewall rules.
  • Explain symmetric and asymmetric encryption and where each is used.
  • Explain hashing, digital signatures and digital certificates.

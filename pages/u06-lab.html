Unit 6 · Virtual Private Network (VPN)

6.7Hands-on lab: Build a VPN

Do these tasks on your own computer or in the lab using free software. Write down what you see at each step: your notes are useful evidence for assessments and for revision.

  1. Build a WireGuard or OpenVPN server in a VM and connect a second VM or phone as a client.
  2. Confirm the client receives a tunnel IP and can reach a private server (ping 10.8.0.1).
  3. Use Wireshark to show that traffic on the physical interface is encrypted.

In Wireshark, capture on the physical network adapter while you ping through the tunnel: you should see only UDP packets to port 51820 (WireGuard) with unreadable contents, not the ICMP pings.

Unit 9 · Network Troubleshooting

9.1Troubleshooting methodology

1Identify the problem→2Theory of cause→3Test the theory→4Plan of action→5Implement→6Verify→7Document
  1. Identify the problem: gather information, ask questions (who, what, when, what changed?), reproduce the fault, define the scope (one user, one subnet, everyone).
  2. Establish a theory of probable cause (start with the simple and common).
  3. Test the theory to confirm; if wrong, form a new theory or escalate.
  4. Establish a plan of action and consider the impact (get approval for production changes).
  5. Implement the solution (change only one thing at a time).
  6. Verify full system functionality and apply preventive measures.
  7. Document findings, actions and outcomes.

Approaches

  • Top-down: start at the application layer and move down.
  • Bottom-up: start at the physical layer and move up (best when nothing works).
  • Divide and conquer: start at a middle layer (usually ping at layer 3) and move up or down depending on the result.
  • Follow the path and compare with a working system; substitute known-good parts.

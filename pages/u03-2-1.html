Unit 3 · Network Devices and Protocols

3.2.1Planning checklist

  1. Draw the network diagram and list the devices and cables needed.
  2. Plan IP addressing (see Unit 4) and VLANs if needed.
  3. Mount equipment, label cables, connect power and uplinks.
  4. Connect to the device console (serial cable and terminal program such as PuTTY at 9600 baud) for first-time setup.
  5. Apply configuration, test, save, and document.

2.8Basic network design and structured cabling

  • Plan for scalability, reliability, security and cost.
  • A common enterprise design has three layers: access (end devices connect), distribution (policy, routing between VLANs) and core (fast backbone). Small networks collapse these into one or two layers.
  • Use structured cabling: work area, horizontal cabling, patch panel, equipment room, backbone.

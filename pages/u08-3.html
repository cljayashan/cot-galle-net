8.3Cryptography basics

Encryption converts readable plaintext into unreadable ciphertext with an algorithm and a key; decryption reverses it. Security depends on the secrecy of the key, not the algorithm.

Cryptography protects data mathematically: secrecy (encryption), integrity (hashing) and strong authentication (public-key signatures). TLS on HTTPS combines these ideas.

Key words in this topic

  • Symmetric: same key encrypts and decrypts.
  • Asymmetric: public encrypts or verifies; private decrypts or signs.
  • Hash: one-way fingerprint of data.
  • Hybrid cryptography: asymmetric to share a symmetric session key (TLS).

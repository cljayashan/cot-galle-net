Unit 6 · Virtual Private Network (VPN)

6.4VPN protocols

ProtocolNotes
IPsec (IKE + ESP/AH)Network-layer standard; common for site-to-site; modes: transport and tunnel
SSL/TLS VPN (e.g. OpenVPN)Uses TLS, works over TCP/UDP 443 or 1194, passes through firewalls easily
WireGuardModern, small and fast, uses UDP (default 51820)
L2TP/IPsecTunnel with IPsec encryption
PPTPObsolete and insecure; avoid

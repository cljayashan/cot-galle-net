Unit 6 · Virtual Private Network (VPN)

6.4VPN protocols

ProtocolNotes
IPsec (IKE + ESP/AH)Network-layer standard; common for site-to-site; modes: transport and tunnel
SSL/TLS VPN (e.g. OpenVPN)Uses TLS, works over TCP/UDP 443 or 1194, passes through firewalls easily
WireGuardModern, small and fast, uses UDP (default 51820)
L2TP/IPsecTunnel with IPsec encryption
PPTPObsolete and insecure; avoid

How to choose quickly

  • Site-to-site between firewalls: IKEv2/IPsec is the usual enterprise choice.
  • Bring-your-own-client remote access with simple config: WireGuard.
  • Need to look like HTTPS to pass strict networks: OpenVPN on TCP 443 (or a TLS VPN portal).
  • Old PPTP profiles in documentation: replace them; do not rebuild them.

Ports must be open on the path between client and gateway. If UDP is blocked, prefer a TCP-based option or fix the middle firewall rather than disabling encryption.

Remember

  • Prefer WireGuard, OpenVPN or modern IPsec (IKEv2).
  • PPTP is obsolete; do not deploy it.
